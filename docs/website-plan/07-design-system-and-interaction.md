# 07 — Design system and interaction

## Creative direction

Refined operational editorial: warm, quiet, precise, and trustworthy. The memorable quality should be the feeling that Nella understands serious work and makes complexity legible.

Avoid the default AI visual language: neon gradients, dark glowing dashboards, floating robot heads, generic rounded cards, excessive glass, and invented metrics.

## Typography

Use a distinctive editorial display face paired with a highly readable sans-serif. Establish a fluid type scale with `clamp()`. Keep body copy comfortable for long-form trust content.

## Colour

Use a warm paper/ink base with one restrained institutional accent. Tint neutrals toward the brand hue. Keep contrast compliant and provide a high-contrast mode if needed.

## Layout

- left-aligned editorial hierarchy;
- varied section rhythm and occasional asymmetry;
- large quiet margins around important claims;
- process diagrams and evidence panels instead of endless cards;
- content width designed for reading, not dashboard density.

## Signature visual

The site’s own product object should be a “work trail”: request, sources, prepared output, approval state, and resulting task. This communicates usefulness and governance at once.

## Interaction principles

- progressive disclosure for technical/privacy detail;
- accordions must preserve deep-linkable headings and keyboard access;
- motion should show status changes and sequence, not decorate every scroll;
- support `prefers-reduced-motion`;
- every demo must work with keyboard and screen reader text;
- never put critical meaning only in colour, animation, or hover.

## Responsive behavior

On mobile, reorder around decision-making: proposition, proof, controls, consultation. Convert timelines to stacked steps and comparison tables to labelled rows. Do not hide trust content or secondary actions just to preserve a desktop composition.
