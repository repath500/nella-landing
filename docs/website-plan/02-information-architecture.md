# 02 — Information architecture

## Launch navigation

- Nella (home)
- How it works
- What it supports
- Privacy and deployment
- About Nella
- Pricing
- Compare
- Resources
- **Book a private consultation**

## Launch pages

### `/`

Positioning, workflow demonstration, capabilities, custom build process, controls, privacy/deployment, proof, and consultation CTA.

### `/how-it-works`

Discovery, system connection, configuration, probation, rollout, governance, and continuous improvement.

### `/what-it-supports`

Six workflow areas: day, inbox, matters, documents, meetings, and research. Each area needs a concrete example, boundary, and outcome.

### `/privacy-and-deployment`

Hosting choices, data boundaries, permissions, retention, model policies, auditability, approvals, and security evidence.

### `/about`

Why Nella exists, relevant company experience, operating philosophy, and people/contact details.

### `/consultation`

Short qualification form plus calendar booking. Explain what the first call covers.

### `/pricing`

Show the commercial shape of the offer without pretending every firm has the same deployment. Include private managed, customer-cloud, and local/sovereign paths; list what changes price; use “from” only when the starting point is real and approved.

### `/compare`

Compare Nella with generic AI, legal AI platforms, and an internal automation project using categorical, evidence-safe language. Focus on implementation, firm-specific configuration, deployment control, integrations, autonomy, and auditability.

### `/resources`

Build trust with practical guides: AI adoption questions, privacy questions for vendors, deployment trade-offs, human oversight, and evaluating assistants on real workflows. Each piece needs an owner and review date.

## Phase-two pages

- `/integrations/[system]`
- `/industries/[practice-area]`
- `/compare`
- `/pricing`
- `/trust-centre`
- jurisdiction pages for Ireland, UK, Germany, and US;
- case studies and implementation stories.

## Avoid at launch

- an “Agents” page;
- a generic AI glossary as the main content strategy;
- a self-serve account or free trial;
- a large product dashboard tour without a real workflow;
- a persona-only product site that obscures Nella.

## Footer architecture

Company, contact, privacy notice, terms, cookie preferences, accessibility, security/trust centre when available, and jurisdiction/legal information. Keep the consultation CTA visible.
