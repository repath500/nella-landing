# 10 — Open decisions before design/build

Resolve these before locking copy or visuals:

1. Is Nella the company, the product, or both in public language?
2. Is the assistant/persona name public, demo-only, or internal?
3. Which countries are launch markets?
4. Which workflows are genuinely live today?
5. Which integrations are live, in pilot, or planned?
6. What deployment models can Nella support operationally today?
7. Which model/provider policies can be implemented today?
8. What exact privacy, retention, logging, and training claims are contractually true?
9. What proof exists: customers, pilots, metrics, testimonials, team background?
10. What pricing can be stated publicly, and what is custom-only?
11. Who owns regulatory, security, and content review?
12. What is the first consultation offer and expected next step?
13. What visual brand assets already exist for Nella?
14. What stack, CMS, hosting, analytics, and form/calendar tools are approved?
15. What is the definition of a qualified lead and how will it be measured?

## Recommended next decision

Write a one-page “truth sheet” listing every live capability, supported integration, deployment option, evidence-backed claim, and planned item. All website copy should be generated from that sheet.
