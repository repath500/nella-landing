# 08 — SEO and localisation

## Search intent

Target practical, high-intent language:

- private AI for law firms;
- virtual assistant for law firms;
- AI assistant for solicitors;
- law-firm workflow automation;
- private/on-premise AI for professional services;
- AI assistant for Outlook or Microsoft Teams in a law firm;
- managed AI implementation for law firms;
- AI for law firm administration.

Do not make “AI agents” the primary category unless search and sales evidence later supports it.

## Content architecture

Create original pages only when Nella can provide meaningful, accurate content. Prioritise workflow pages, deployment explainers, trust content, and case studies over thin location pages.

## Localisation requirements

### Ireland

Use solicitor/law-firm terminology, EUR pricing, and relevant local data-protection and professional context. Validate all references before publishing.

### United Kingdom

Use solicitor/law-firm terminology and UK-specific operational language. Do not turn current regulator commentary into evergreen claims without a review process.

### Germany

Use professional German translation and review, not machine translation alone. Bring confidentiality, residency, provider control, and local deployment forward.

### United States

Use attorney/law-firm language, explain firm supervision and confidentiality, and avoid implying that Nella provides legal services.

## Technical SEO

Semantic headings, descriptive metadata, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots rules, Open Graph cards, structured data for Organisation/Service/FAQ where accurate, fast media, accessible transcripts, and a clear cookie/privacy implementation.
