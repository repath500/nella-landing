# 01 — Brand and positioning

## Recommended position

**Nella builds private, managed digital support systems for law firms.**

Short version:

> More support for the work that surrounds your lawyers.

Expanded version:

> Nella builds and manages a private assistant system around your firm’s people, documents, systems, and way of working—so your team can spend less time finding, preparing, chasing, and updating.

## Brand character

- calm, exact, discreet, and capable;
- human and plain-spoken, without pretending the technology is human;
- operationally serious rather than futuristic;
- confident about control and boundaries;
- editorial and considered, not loud SaaS marketing.

## Words to use

private, managed, firm-specific, support, prepare, find, organise, review, approve, accountable, controlled, connected, practical, secure by design, built around your way of working.

## Words to avoid or constrain

autonomous agents, AI lawyer, replace your paralegals, magic, effortless, guaranteed, hallucination-free, fully secure, unlimited, revolutionary, chatbot, generic legal intelligence.

Technical terms such as orchestration, RAG, vector database, MCP, model routing, and tool calling belong in technical documentation only.

## Messaging pillars

### Built around your firm

Nella learns approved processes, templates, permissions, terminology, and systems. The firm should not have to reshape itself around a generic tool.

### Works where work already happens

Email, Microsoft Teams, approved messaging, calendars, document repositories, practice-management systems, and a small admin workspace where needed.

### Control is part of the product

Show what the system may read, prepare, change, send, or never do. Make approval, provenance, logging, and permissions tangible.

### Private by deployment choice

Offer a managed private environment, deployment in the firm’s cloud, or a local/sovereign option where technically and commercially appropriate.

### Nella stays accountable for the implementation

Discovery, configuration, evaluation, rollout, monitoring, and improvement are part of the service.

## Suggested homepage line

> **Nella gives your firm its own private support system.**

Supporting line:

> Built around your people, connected to the systems you already use, and managed with the controls professional work requires.
