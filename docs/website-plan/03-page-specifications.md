# 03 — Page specifications

## Homepage

Purpose: make Nella understandable in under 30 seconds and credible enough to book.

Required sections:

1. Nella proposition and primary CTA.
2. Realistic message/workflow demonstration.
3. “Support around lawyers, not a replacement for judgement.”
4. Six workflow areas.
5. Built around the firm.
6. Nella’s implementation process.
7. Autonomy and approval controls.
8. Privacy and deployment choices.
9. Model/provider policy.
10. Proof and operating credibility.
11. Consultation close.

## How it works

Use an employee-onboarding metaphor without pretending Nella is an employee:

1. Understand the firm.
2. Connect approved systems.
3. Configure workflows and boundaries.
4. Test against representative work.
5. Roll out with review and approval controls.
6. Measure, monitor, and improve.

Include deliverables, customer responsibilities, typical decision points, and what determines timeline/cost.

## What it supports

For each workflow area, show: common request, Nella’s preparation/action, human checkpoint, systems involved, and measurable benefit.

- **Your day:** briefings, diary preparation, reminders, follow-ups.
- **Your inbox:** thread summaries, matter identification, drafts, tasks.
- **Your matters:** retrieval, chronologies, status checks, matter briefs.
- **Your documents:** approved-template drafts, comparison, extraction, precedents.
- **Your meetings:** preparation, dictated notes, attendance notes, follow-up actions.
- **Your research:** sourced packs and review-ready findings, never unreviewed legal advice.

## Privacy and deployment

Explain three patterns without promising that every feature works in every pattern:

- Nella-managed private environment;
- Nella deployed in customer-controlled cloud;
- local/sovereign deployment subject to capability, infrastructure, support, and cost assessment.

Add a decision guide: privacy, capability, latency, cost, residency, operations, and provider policy.

## About

Answer “who will run this inside our firm?” Include named people, relevant experience, operating principles, and evidence-backed company history. Do not use unsupported scale claims.

## Pricing

Qualify rather than bargain. Present three operating models, what affects cost, what implementation includes, and what requires custom assessment. Do not lead with salary replacement. An ROI tool is phase two and must use transparent assumptions.

## Compare

Compare Nella to generic AI, legal AI platforms, and internal automation by category. Use “varies by provider” where evidence differs. Never imply a named competitor is insecure or incapable without a defensible, current source.

## Resources

Use resources to answer the questions buyers ask before contacting Nella. Start with a small number of genuinely useful pieces rather than a high-volume SEO blog. Every resource needs a source, owner, review date, and update/expiry rule.

## Consultation

Heading: **Let’s design the right first deployment for your firm.**

Form fields: name, firm, role, country, approximate team size, current systems, biggest workflow bottleneck, deployment/security requirement, and preferred time. Keep the form short and privacy-conscious.
