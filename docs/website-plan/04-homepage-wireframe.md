# 04 — Homepage wireframe and content flow

## 1. Header

Nella wordmark, four or five links, and a persistent **Book a private consultation** button. On mobile, retain the CTA in the menu and as a bottom action only if it remains accessible and non-intrusive.

## 2. Hero

> **Nella gives your firm its own private support system.**

> Built around your people, documents, systems, and way of working. Nella helps your team find, prepare, organise, and follow through—where work already happens.

Primary CTA: **Book a private consultation**

Secondary CTA: **See how it works**

Quiet proof line: `Firm-specific · Managed by Nella · Deployment designed around your requirements`

Visual: a split-screen “request → prepared work → approval” sequence, not a generic chat bubble. Make provenance, source references, and approval state visible.

## 3. Workflow proof

Show three concrete requests:

- “Prepare my morning matter brief.”
- “Find what is still outstanding and draft the follow-up.”
- “Turn this meeting note into an attendance note and tasks.”

Each result should include source context, a human checkpoint, and an action state.

## 4. Boundary section

> Nella supports the work around professional judgement. It does not replace it.

Use a two-column contrast: what Nella can prepare/organise versus what remains with the responsible professional.

## 5. Capability section

Use six editorial bands rather than a repeated icon grid. Give each one a real scenario and a short outcome.

## 6. Customisation section

> Your firm should not have to learn a generic tool’s way of working.

Show templates, permissions, terminology, systems, and approval rules feeding a firm-specific deployment.

## 7. Implementation section

Show the six-step process as a horizontal timeline on desktop and stacked sequence on mobile. Include “probation” as a controlled test phase, not as a cute metaphor.

## 8. Control section

Use an autonomy/approval matrix:

| Action | Default treatment |
|---|---|
| Find authorised document | Automatic |
| Prepare meeting brief | Automatic |
| Create internal task | Configurable |
| Draft external email | Reviewable |
| Send external email | Approval required |
| Submit a filing or transfer funds | Disabled/Never |

## 9. Privacy/deployment section

Three deployment choices with a concise caveat: exact capabilities depend on architecture, integrations, model policy, and infrastructure.

## 10. Proof and CTA

Use substantiated experience, process evidence, testimonials, pilot outcomes, or a clear “evidence available in consultation” statement. End with the same consultation CTA.
