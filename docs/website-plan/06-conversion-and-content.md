# 06 — Conversion and content system

## Primary conversion

**Book a private consultation**

This is a deployment conversation, not a self-serve signup. Keep the CTA consistent across pages.

## Secondary conversion

**See a private demo** can open the consultation flow with “demo” preselected. Avoid a separate low-intent lead path unless there is a real asset to deliver.

## Consultation promise

Explain exactly what happens:

- understand the firm and its highest-friction workflows;
- map current systems and constraints;
- discuss deployment and provider policy;
- identify a sensible first pilot;
- outline evidence, timeline, and next decision.

## Lead qualification fields

Firm name, country, role, team size, current practice-management/document systems, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, highest-friction workflow, deployment preference, and contact details.

## Content pillars

### Practical workflow stories

“From a forwarded email to a review-ready follow-up.” Use fictionalised data and label it clearly.

### Governance that people can understand

Explain approvals, provenance, permissions, and the difference between preparing and acting.

### Deployment decisions

Compare managed private, customer cloud, and local options without treating one as universally best.

### Implementation knowledge

Explain discovery, testing, rollout, evaluation, and change management.

### Responsible AI for professional teams

Plain-language guidance, reviewed for accuracy, with dates and sources where claims depend on current rules.

## Editorial rules

- one audience and one job per page;
- use concrete examples over feature adjectives;
- write “can” and “is configured to” where capability varies;
- label examples, plans, and estimates;
- keep a review date on legal, security, pricing, and integration content;
- do not publish content that creates a legal-advice expectation.
