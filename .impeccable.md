## Design Context

### Users
Law-firm leaders, partners, operations teams, and technology decision-makers evaluating a private AI support system. They need to understand Nella's value, controls, deployment options, and next commercial step quickly and with confidence.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, exacting. Nella should feel like a discreet operating partner: technically capable, professionally accountable, and clear about what happens next.

### Aesthetic Direction
A refined editorial website with warm paper tones, clay accents, italic Instrument Serif display typography, IBM Plex Sans body copy, and selective cinematic photography. The interface is light-led, tactile, and contemporary. Avoid generic enterprise SaaS gradients, excessive cards, vague caveats, oversized empty gaps, or playful AI mascots.

### Design Principles
- Lead with direct, specific language and visible next actions.
- Use generous but controlled spacing; every section should feel intentional rather than empty.
- Pair cinematic imagery with restrained overlays that preserve legibility and atmosphere.
- Keep controls, human review, and accountability visible without turning the interface into compliance copy.
- Preserve accessible contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and responsive composition.
